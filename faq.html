Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before starting your campaign.

What exactly is book placement?+

Book placement is the process of identifying reading communities, book clubs, and audiences where your book genuinely fits, followed by personalized outreach to introduce it to them.

Do you guarantee book sales?+

No. We do not guarantee sales, bestseller status, or reviews. We create strategic opportunities for discovery and engagement. Final decisions belong to readers and communities.

What genres do you work with?+

We work across many genres, including fiction, memoir, romance, inspirational, self help, YA, mystery, and more. Our specialty is finding the right audience for your specific book, not focusing on one genre alone.

How long does a campaign last?+

Signature campaigns run 30 days, Platinum campaigns run 90 days, and Imperial partnerships run 6 months. Each is structured to allow enough time for meaningful outreach and follow up.

Do I work directly with Amanda?+

Yes. Amanda is personally involved in strategy, outreach direction, and communication throughout every campaign, regardless of package level.

What do I receive at the end of a campaign?+

You will receive a detailed campaign report covering outreach activity, communities contacted, engagement, opportunities identified, and strategic recommendations for next steps.

How do payments work?+

Signature campaigns are paid 100% upfront. Platinum campaigns are split 50/50 across the campaign. Imperial partnerships follow an agreed milestone based schedule.

Do you work with first time authors?+

Yes. We work with independent, first time, hybrid, and traditionally published authors, as long as there is a genuine, finished, professionally presented book ready for promotion.

Which markets do you currently serve?+

Amanda Gary Literary currently works with authors and reading communities across the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia. Additional markets are considered on a case by case basis.

How do I get started?+

Simply fill out our contact form with details about your book and goals. Amanda will personally review your submission and respond within 1 to 2 business days.

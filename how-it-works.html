Our Process

A Strategic, Seven Step Method

Every campaign, regardless of size, follows the same disciplined structure.

Step 01

Discover

We begin by understanding your book, your message, your goals, and what success looks like for you as an author.

Step 02

Position

We identify your book's strongest positioning, genre, tone, comparable titles, and the audience most likely to connect with it.

Step 03

Research

We research relevant communities, readers, clubs, creators and opportunities aligned with your book's positioning.

Step 04

Match

We connect your book with audiences where genuine relevance exists, never generic, mass blasted outreach.

Step 05

Reach

Personalized outreach begins, with thoughtful follow up throughout the campaign period.

Step 06

Engage

We encourage meaningful opportunities for conversation, discovery, and reader engagement around your book.

Step 07

Report

You receive campaign intelligence, activity updates, and strategic recommendations for what comes next.

Throughout

Personal Involvement

Amanda is personally involved in strategy, outreach direction, and communication with you at every stage.

Why This Process Matters

"Strategy before outreach. Research before promotion. Every time."
